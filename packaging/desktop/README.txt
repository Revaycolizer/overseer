Overseer desktop — Apple Silicon preview

This preview has an ad hoc signature. It is not Developer ID signed or notarized
by Apple. It is not a stable release. Intel Macs are not supported by this package.

1. Extract the ZIP and move Overseer.app into Applications.
2. Open Overseer.app. If macOS blocks an unidentified developer or an app that
   Apple cannot check, dismiss the alert and open System Settings > Privacy &
   Security. If you trust this download, choose Open Anyway for Overseer and
   confirm. The option may only be available shortly after the opening attempt.
   A managed Mac may prevent this; contact its administrator.
   Apple instructions: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445
3. Select Ollama or a cloud provider in first-run setup and test the connection.
   Ollama and its models are separate installations. Cloud use may incur fees.

The app includes its backend; no separate server command is needed. Closing its
window keeps it in the tray; use Quit to stop the app and its owned backend.
Optional voice and desktop control may require separate setup and permissions.

Checksums detect download corruption; they do not verify publisher identity.
Do not disable Gatekeeper or remove quarantine attributes to install this preview.
If macOS reports damage or malware, stop and report the exact message.
Uninstall by removing Overseer.app. Your saved data remains on this Mac.
